# Rostam & Sohrab: the story in 20 rows, 40 parts

Source: Ferdowsi's Shahnameh, the story of Rostam and Sohrab (Ganjoor: https://ganjoor.net/ferdousi/shahname/sohrab/).
Each row below is about 5 minutes of story, told as **two parts of under 3 minutes**: row r = parts 2r−1 and 2r.
Rows 1–2 are parts 1–4, done (`ep01/`, cut with `split_n.py`): 1 «رستم», 2 «رخش», 3 «ربودن رخش», 4 «مهمان سمنگان».
Every part's title is in `parts.json` ("titles").

| row | row title | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Lost Horse · رخش گمشده | Cold open (Sohrab: "Are you Rostam?" — Rostam: "No."). Ferdowsi and his question about fate (the unripe citron). Rostam's birth and deeds; how he won Rakhsh as a colt. Iran and Turan. Rostam hunts near Samangan, roasts a wild ass, sleeps by a stream. Turanian riders find giant hoofprints. |
| 2 | The Stolen Horse · ربودن رخش | The riders lasso Rakhsh after a fight and take him to Samangan. Rostam wakes, finds the tracks, walks to Samangan with his saddle. The king welcomes him, promises to find Rakhsh, hosts a feast. Tahmineh listens behind a lattice. Night: a maid with a candle leads someone to Rostam's door. |
| 3 | Tahmineh · تهمینه | Tahmineh enters at midnight with the maid; Rostam wakes. She tells him who she is (the king of Samangan's daughter), that she has heard of his deeds and loves him, that she wants a son like him, and promises to bring Rakhsh back. Rostam is moved; he sends for a wise priest (mobed) to ask the king for her hand. |
| 4 | Rostam's Armband · مهره‌ی رستم | The king gives his consent; the wedding that night. Rostam gives Tahmineh the famous jewel from his arm: "If it is a girl, tie it in her hair; if a boy, on his arm." Morning: Rakhsh is found; Rostam leaves for Zabol. Tahmineh's sorrow. Nine months later a son is born: Sohrab. |
| 5 | Whose Son Am I? · پسر کیستم؟ | Sohrab grows fast and strong (at ten no one can wrestle him). He asks his mother who his father is, and threatens her when she hesitates. She tells him: Rostam, son of Zal; shows him Rostam's letter/gifts; warns him to hide it from Afrasiab. |
| 6 | Sohrab's Army · لشکر سهراب | Sohrab's dream: march on Iran, take King Kavus's throne and give it to Rostam, then overthrow Afrasiab. He looks for a horse strong enough for him and finds a colt of Rakhsh's line. He gathers an army. |
| 7 | Afrasiab's Plot · نیرنگ افراسیاب | Afrasiab hears of Sohrab, sends Hooman and Barman with an army and gifts, and secretly orders them: never let father and son recognise each other; if Rostam dies, then kill Sohrab too. |
| 8 | The White Fortress · دژ سپید | Sohrab reaches the White Fortress on Iran's border. Hojir rides out to fight him; Sohrab defeats him and takes him prisoner. |
| 9 | Gordafarid · گردآفرید | Gordafarid, Gazhdaham's daughter, rides out in armour and fights Sohrab; her helmet falls and he sees she is a woman. She tricks him with a promise and slips back into the fortress. |
| 10 | The Empty Fortress · دژ خالی | From the wall Gordafarid mocks Sohrab. Gazhdaham writes to King Kavus about the young giant; at night the people leave through a secret passage. In the morning Sohrab finds the fortress empty. |
| 11 | A Letter to the King · نامه به کاووس | Kavus reads the letter and sends Giv to Zabol for Rostam. Rostam laughs at the idea of a Turanian like Sam, feasts and drinks for days before setting out. |
| 12 | The Angry King · خشم کاووس | Kavus, furious at the delay, orders Tus to hang Rostam and Giv. Rostam storms out ("I am the crown-giver"). Gudarz goes after him and brings him back for Iran's sake; the king apologises; the army marches. |
| 13 | Night in the Enemy Camp · شبی در اردوی دشمن | Rostam, disguised as a Turk, enters the enemy camp at night and sees Sohrab at a feast, as tall and strong as Sam. Zhendeh Razm, Tahmineh's brother (sent to recognise Rostam), catches him; Rostam kills him with one blow and slips away. |
| 14 | Hojir's Lie · دروغ هجیر | Morning: from a hill Sohrab asks the prisoner Hojir to name the Iranian commanders by their tents and banners. At the green tent with the dragon banner Hojir lies: "a Chinese ally"; he fears Sohrab will kill Rostam. Sohrab's heart is uneasy. |
| 15 | The First Duel · نبرد نخست | Sohrab attacks the Iranian camp; Rostam comes out. Sohrab asks "Are you Rostam?" (the scene of part 1's cold open, from the start); Rostam denies it. They fight with spears, swords and maces until their weapons break. |
| 16 | The First Night · شب نخست | Bows and arrows, then belt-grappling on horseback; Sohrab's mace hits Rostam's shoulder. Night ends the fight. In his tent Rostam tells his brother Zavareh: if I die, comfort my mother and take my body to Zabol. Sohrab asks Hooman about the old warrior. |
| 17 | The Wrestling · کشتی | Second day: Sohrab, smiling, asks to make peace and asks again if he is Rostam; Rostam refuses. They wrestle; Sohrab throws Rostam and sits on his chest with a dagger. Rostam tricks him ("our custom: you must throw a man twice before you kill him"). Sohrab lets him go. |
| 18 | The Armband · بازوبند | Rostam washes at a stream and prays for his old strength. They wrestle again; Rostam throws Sohrab and stabs him. Dying, Sohrab says his father Rostam will avenge him. Rostam opens the armour and sees the jewel he gave Tahmineh. |
| 19 | The Antidote · نوشدارو | Rostam's grief. He sends Gudarz to Kavus for the antidote (nooshdaru); Kavus refuses (a son like this would make Rostam too strong). Rostam rides to fetch it himself; on the way he learns that Sohrab has died. |
| 20 | The Mourning · سوگ | Rostam tries to kill himself; the nobles stop him. He burns his tent and carries Sohrab's body to Zabol in a coffin. Tahmineh's lament (she dies of grief a year later). Ferdowsi's closing lines. |

## Continuity notes

- **Rostam**: domed helmet with a plume, long beard, tiger-skin mantle (Babr-e Bayan), ox-head mace; huge
  (`rostam`; `rostam_bare` without the helmet, `rostam_robe` at home or at night). Voice am_fenrir.
- **Rakhsh**: big stallion, in silhouette; give him a rose rim light when he must stand out.
- **Sohrab**: tall, beardless, long hair, pointed helmet with a tall plume, cape, spear (`sohrab`). Voice am_puck.
  As a child: `sohrab` at scale 0.6–0.75.
- **Tahmineh**: veil with a small crown, long dress (`tahmineh`). Voice af_heart. The maid: `maid`, voice bf_isabella.
- **Sohrab as a boy** (parts 9–10): `young_sohrab` (bare head, long hair, tunic), ~1.4 next to Tahmineh at 1.5;
  the jewel glows on his upper arm (`gem_at(c, "s:hand_up", …)`). Other children: `boy` (voice af_sky, role BOY).
  Part 10: Tahmineh told him he is Rostam's son, showed Rostam's letter and three rubies set in gold, and warned
  him to hide it from Afrasiab; Rostam knows he has a son but thinks he is still a little child.
- **King of Samangan**: crown with ribbons, long beard, robe, cape (`king`). Kavus and Afrasiab: `kavus`, `afrasiab`.
- **Gordafarid**: `gordafarid` (female face, pointed helmet, plume, hair, armour). Hojir: `warrior_ir`.
  Turanians: `warrior_tu` / `rider`.
- The jewel/armband (مهره): Rostam gives it in part 4; it is hidden under Sohrab's armour until part 18.
- Part 29 (row 15, "Are You Rostam?") starts with the same exchange as part 1's cold open (you can reuse `anim.cold_open`'s world).
- **Parts 11–12**: Sohrab is still `young_sohrab` (the jewel glowing on his upper arm) until the army gathers; from
  part 12's army shot (S07) on he is `sohrab` (armour; the jewel hidden under it until part 18). His horse: a wild
  colt of Rakhsh's line from the high pastures (a `horse` at ~0.8 next to him, rose rim `COLT` in p11_scenes),
  saddled from part 12 S04. The king of Samangan gave him gold, weapons and horses; when the war drums sounded,
  Tahmineh, in tears, told him to keep the jewel on his arm ("a keepsake from your father... one day you will need
  it"); he told her he is going to find his father. His plan (part 11): take Kavus's throne and crown for Rostam,
  then Afrasiab's ("when the sun and the moon shine, why should the stars wear crowns?"). Part 12 ends in
  Afrasiab's dark palace (`dark_hall`, `sit_throne`, red firelight): a messenger brings the news and Afrasiab says
  "The son of Rostam... against Iran? Good. Very good." Part 13 can open there.
- **Parts 13-14** (`p13_scenes.py`): Hooman (`HOOMAN`: fur cap, long beard, cape, mace; voice am_eric) and Barman
  (`BARMAN`: fur cap, short beard, spear; voice am_santa) are Afrasiab's commanders. Part 13: the backstory (young
  Rostam once lifted Afrasiab out of the saddle by the belt; the belt broke and his men saved him), Afrasiab sends them
  with 12,000 riders, gifts (gold, horses, a throne and a crown) and a sweet, lying letter ("take the throne of Iran...
  Samangan, Iran and Turan will be one"). Part 14: his secret order (father and son must never know each other; if
  Rostam dies, kill Sohrab in his sleep), the march at dawn, Sohrab's welcome in his camp (Hooman amazed at his arms and
  shoulders), the letter read aloud; Sohrab trusts them. It ends at sunset ("Tomorrow, we march to the border of
  Iran!") and on the White Fortress under the moon (`white_fortress()`): part 15 can open there. No verses (Ganjoor
  fetch refused in the scheduled run).
- **Parts 15-16** (`p15_scenes.py`, `p16_scenes.py`): the White Fortress on its hill on Iran's border (`fortress_far`, `wall_top`, `gate_court`, `plain_below`/`field`). Its keeper Hojir (`HOJIR` = warrior_ir, spear; voice am_michael); old Gazhdaham (`GAZH`: turban, long beard, cape, staff; voice bm_lewis at 0.84, role GAZHDAHAM) and his daughter Gordafarid (`GORD`, dress and veil; voice bf_emma) live there. Part 15: the host arrives at dawn; Hojir rides out alone; face to face ("Who are you...?" -- "I am Hojir... I will send your head to King Kavus!"); Sohrab laughs; they charge. Part 16: Hojir's lance hits Sohrab's belt and he does not move; Sohrab turns his lance and throws him from the saddle with its butt, kneels on his chest with a dagger; Hojir begs for mercy; Sohrab spares him ("never boast before a battle") and sends him bound to Hooman (Barman: "a prisoner who knows Iran can be useful" -- he is kept for part 28). The fortress laments; Gordafarid, ashamed and angry: "I will, Father... Bring me my armour and my horse." Part 16 ends on her putting on her helmet (`GORD_ARM`, the "gordafarid" look): part 17 can open there. No verses (Ganjoor fetch refused in the scheduled run).
- **Parts 17-18** (`p17_scenes.py`, `p18_scenes.py`): Gordafarid in armour is `GORD_HID` (helmeted, hair hidden: the Turanians take her for a man), `GORD_BARE` once Sohrab has pulled her helmet off; her horse has the morning rim, Sohrab's colt the rose one. Part 17: she hides her hair and ties on her helmet in the gate court (Gazhdaham: "He is a young giant"), rides out and calls out the host ("Where are your heroes?"); Sohrab laughs ("Another prey walks into my trap"; Hooman: "Shall I send one of our men?" -- "This one is mine"); her rain of arrows, his shield over his head (`shield()`); his spear strikes her belt and lifts her out of the saddle (`lifted()`), she cuts it in two with her sword and drops back; she flees, he pulls off her helmet: her hair falls loose, "A girl?!". Part 18: his lasso round her waist (`lasso_throw()`, `loop_at()`); her trick (two armies are watching, his men would laugh, make peace quietly, the fortress and its treasure will be his, come to the gate); Sohrab, charmed ("don't break your promise... those walls are not higher than the clouds; one blow of my mace and they fall"), rides with her to the gate; the doors open a little, she slips in, the gate crashes shut in his face (`gate_outside()`); inside, Gazhdaham: "My brave lioness... you tricked him too!" -- "A lion's strength was not enough, Father. So I used a fox's trick." It ends with Sohrab at the closed gate hearing laughter, and Gordafarid on the wall against the sky, laughing (`wall_low()`): part 19 opens with her mockery from the wall. No verses (Ganjoor fetch refused in the scheduled run).
- **Parts 19-20** (`p19_scenes.py`, `p20_scenes.py`): Part 19: Gordafarid on the wall (`GORD_BARE`) mocks Sohrab ("Why so sad, Turk? Did you think a girl of Iran would be yours?"), then warns him: "I don't think you are a Turk at all... no hero is your equal... but our king will send Rostam, and no one can stand against Rostam" (a vision: Rostam's giant shadow in the sky) "...go back to Turan"; Sohrab, ashamed: "Laugh while you can... tomorrow I will tear down these walls" and rides back to camp. At sunset on the wall Gazhdaham fears the walls won't hold and calls a scribe; his letter to Kavus (told as shadows on the curtain): a young leader, almost a boy, taller than a cypress, face like the sun, named Sohrab, Hojir is his prisoner, "when he rides you would think he is Sam, Rostam's grandfather, come back to life"; "if the king waits, Iran is lost". A rider leaves with the letter at midnight; Gazhdaham: "Tonight we leave the fortress... there is a secret way under it". Part 20: the people leave by night through the secret passage with lamps held low (a boy is scared; Gordafarid holds his hand) and come out before dawn in the hills behind the fortress ("Goodbye, White Fortress" -- "Come. The road to Iran is long."). At sunrise the war drums; Sohrab: "the girl is mine, bring her to me alive" (Hooman with him); the walls are silent, he smashes the gate with his mace: the fortress is empty ("She promised me this fortress... and she kept her word"); the pain in his heart, he cannot forget her face (her laughing shadow in the sky). Ends with Sohrab on the wall ("Run to your king. I am coming, all the way to his throne") and the messenger galloping into the sunset toward Kavus: part 21 can open at Kavus's court with the letter. No verses (Ganjoor fetch refused in the scheduled run).
