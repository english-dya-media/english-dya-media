# What works on @english_dya

Written by the daily check, read by the weekly run before it writes a week. Keep it under ~40 lines.
Goal: **+5,000 followers a month** (≈ +167 a day net). Numbers: `python3 growth/update.py`.

## Where we are
- Start of November: ~64.7K followers, net −10 to −20 a day (16–29 unfollows vs 5–12 follows a day). The page was
  quiet before 3 Nov; unfollows are slowly falling since daily posting started.
- 9 Nov: 64,685 followers (−12 vs 8 Nov); November so far −86, last 7 days −12/day → needs +242/day for the rest of
  the month. 8 Nov: +13 follows (best day yet) / −19 unfollows. Unfollows 16–29/day vs 6–13 follows.
- Daily reels reach 300–1,560 people, ~760 views on average, and **0 shares** on any reel so far. That reach is mostly
  followers: the page needs reels that get pushed to non-followers.

## Reels so far
- Best: day04 «با ت و س نگو» (th: three, think, month, clothes): 1,699 views, 43.9% view rate, 6.1 s watch, 10 saves.
  A sound Iranians replace with a Persian one, words everyone uses → more of this kind (w/v, final -ng, "short i vs ee").
- Best (updated 10 Nov): day04 now 1,783 views, 43.1%, 5.9 s, 10 saves — still 2× any other reel.
- day07 -ed endings (walked, played, stopped, cleaned), 9 Nov, after ~19 h: 365 views, 34.8%, 5.5 s watch, 3 saves and
  the **first share** of any daily reel. Watch time 2nd only to day04 → grammar-sound rules (-ed) hold attention.
- Worst: day06 silent b (climb, thumb, lamb, debt): 348 views, 27.7%, 3.9 s. day05 (-ology words): 423 views, 22.8%.
- Silent letters (day01, day03, day06): 350–840 views, 28–38% view rate — falling with each repeat. Extra syllable (day02): 556, 31%.
- Trial reels: trial06 (mistakes, 8 Nov) 124 views, 33.0%, 4.6 s, 2 saves — barely grew on day 2 (123 → 124).
  trial07 (vocab "instead of very", 9 Nov) 127 views, 32.5%, 4.7 s, 3 saves, 1 share. Both stall at ~120 reach (vs
  ~740 avg views for daily reels); no format difference yet. Their saves/shares per view are higher than daily reels.
- Promo reel "follow for 4 words a day" (8 Nov 11:30): 508 views, 28.2%; 8 Nov had the most follows yet (+13), still net −6.
  A second promo reel went out 10 Nov 11:30 (no numbers yet).
- Viewers leave early: avg watch 3.4–6.1 s of a 26 s reel, under half watch past 3 s.

## For the next weekly run

- Use silent letters at most once a week: three silent-letter reels so far, views falling (837 → 624 → 348).
