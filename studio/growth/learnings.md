# What works on @english_dya

Written by the daily check, read by the weekly run before it writes a week. Keep it under ~40 lines.
Goal: **+5,000 followers a month** (≈ +167 a day net). Numbers: `python3 growth/update.py`.

## Where we are
- Start of November: ~64.7K followers, net −10 to −20 a day. The page was quiet before 3 Nov.
- 11 Nov: 64,661 followers (−15 vs 10 Nov); November so far −110, last 7 days −11.1/day → needs +269/day for the
  rest of the month. 10 Nov: +15 follows / **−31 unfollows** (most unfollows yet). Follows 7–18/day (rising a little),
  unfollows 16–31/day (not falling). Net still negative every day.
- Daily reels reach 280–1,600 people (~700 views avg); shares are rare (day07 2, day09 2; others 0). Reach is mostly
  followers: the page needs reels that get pushed to non-followers.

## Reels so far (views, view rate, avg watch)
- Best: day04 th → ت/س (three, think, month, clothes): 1,811 views, 42.7%, 5.8 s, 11 saves — still 2× any other reel.
  A sound Iranians replace, words everyone uses.
- day07 -ed endings: 518, 33.0%, 4.9 s, 4 saves, 2 shares → sound rules hold attention and get shared.
- day09 silent h (hour, honest, vehicle, exhausted), 11 Nov, after ~19 h: 334 views, 34.2%, 4.8 s, 4 saves, 2 shares.
  Better start than day08 at 18 h (287) but 5th silent-letter reel, reach still well below the first ones.
- day08 silent l: 424 views at ~43 h (287 at 18 h), 35.9%, 5.3 s.
- Worst: day05 (-ology words) 510 views, 23.0%, 3.3 s; day06 silent b 500, 25.5%, 3.8 s.
- Silent letters (day01/03/06/08/09): 886 → 702 → 500 → 424 → 334 views — each repeat does worse.
- Trial reels: 4 so far, all stall at 113–122 reach / 122–133 views (vs ~700 for daily reels). Mistakes: trial06 124
  (33.0%), trial08 122 (40.5%, best rate). Vocab: trial07 133 (31.7%), trial09 «very scared/angry» 127 (28.1%, 3.7 s,
  3 saves, 2 shares). No format gap in views; mistakes hold viewers better (33–40% vs 28–32%).
  Saves+shares per view are 2–4× daily reels: people who see them value them, Instagram just doesn't widen them.
- Promos: presenter (8 Nov) 551 views, 28.7%; presenter2 (10 Nov) 456 views, 28.2%, 7 saves, 3 shares.
- Viewers leave early: avg watch 3.3–5.8 s, under half watch past 3 s.
