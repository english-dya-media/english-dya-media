# What works on @english_dya

Written by the daily check, read by the weekly run before it writes a week. Keep it under ~50 lines.
Goal: **+5,000 followers a month** (≈ +167 a day net). Numbers: `python3 growth/update.py`.

## Where we are
- 21 Nov: 64,568 followers (−11 vs 20 Nov); November so far −203, last 7 days −9.6/day → would need +578/day for the
  last 9 days. Net negative every day since 2 Nov. Follows rose a little (+22/−27 on 19 Nov, +13/−21 on 20 Nov vs
  +5–10 on 14–18 Nov) but unfollows rose with them (21–27/day on 18–20 Nov vs 13–17 before).
  Account reach 1,280–1,610 a day on 14–20 Nov vs 525–757 on 7–11 Nov; reels settle at ~400–550 views.

## YouTube (every video as a Short since 14 Nov 18:10 Rome)
- Dormant before (1–17 views a day); 97% of views from the Shorts feed, 93% new viewers (Studio, 15 Nov).
- Subscribers (Metricool): 117 (14 Nov) → 121 → 140 → 155 → 175 (18 Nov) → 177 (20 Nov) → 176 (21 Nov): **growth
  stopped after 18 Nov** (+2 then −1). Channel views 2,500–3,400 a day on 15–18 Nov, then 1,064 on 19 Nov.
  The stall began while the backlog copies were still going out (8–9 Shorts a day); 4 a day from 21 Nov.
- Pronunciation reels win on YouTube: day08 silent l 1,154 views (IG 576), day04 1,124, day06 silent b 976 (IG 563),
  day01 616, day13 529. Silent letters (day08, day06) get ~2× their IG views here.
- Trial reels do better on YouTube than IG (avg 217 vs 140; trial16a 405 vs 155, trial09 331 vs 128); trial15a got
  220 on YouTube vs 27 on IG.
- Quote reels are swiped away (avg 109 views, 1–7 s average view, vs ~570 on IG). Rostam parts get 8–81 views on the
  feed (avg 44) though viewers watch 11–38 s: long videos are not pushed as Shorts.
- Shorts of 19 Nov (day17 141, trial17a 106, rostam10/11 8 each) are far below 15–18 Nov — matches the stall.

## For the next weekly run
- Pronunciation themes with everyday words are the best daily format on both platforms (day04, day08, day06 on
  YouTube; day04, day10 on IG). Prefer sounds Iranians replace and odd vowels; avoid rule themes (-ed, plural -s).
- Silent letters: weak on IG but best on YouTube — at most one a week, with very common words (salmon, half, walk).
- Rostam/cartoon parts are now the best IG reels (~620 views) but get almost nothing on YouTube: keep two a day on
  IG and the playlist; don't count on them for YouTube subscribers.
- YouTube subscribers flat since 18 Nov and only 4 Shorts a day now: consider re-cutting the best pronunciation
  reels (day04, day08, day06, day01) as extra YouTube Shorts rather than posting quote reels there.
- Quote reels: drop them from YouTube (avg 109 views) or replace with a one-word-explained version; keep on IG.
- Trial reels: ~1 in 8 gets ~25 views (trial15a, trial19a, both 12:30 Rome vocab slot) — check in IG that the
  12:30 trial is being shown to non-followers; otherwise keep both formats.
