# What works on @english_dya

Written by the daily check, read by the weekly run before it writes a week. Keep it under ~40 lines.
Goal: **+5,000 followers a month** (≈ +167 a day net). Numbers: `python3 growth/update.py`.

## Where we are
- Start of November: ~64.7K followers, net −10 to −20 a day. The page was quiet before 3 Nov.
- 12 Nov: 64,654 followers (−7 vs 11 Nov); November so far −117, last 7 days −10.1/day → needs +284/day for the
  rest of the month. 11 Nov: +14 follows / −24 unfollows. Follows 7–18/day, unfollows 16–31/day (not falling).
  Net still negative every day. Account reach 12 Nov 901 (highest since 6 Nov), the day the quote reel started.
- Daily reels reach 280–1,600 people (~700 views avg); shares are rare (day07 2, day09 2; others 0). Reach is mostly
  followers: the page needs reels that get pushed to non-followers.

## Reels so far (views, view rate, avg watch)
- Best: day04 th → ت/س (three, think, month, clothes): 1,811 views, 42.7%, 5.8 s, 11 saves — still 2× any other reel.
  A sound Iranians replace, words everyone uses.

  3 saves. Faster start than day09 (334 at 19 h) and day08 (287 at 18 h). Odd-vowel words hold viewers.

  **trial10 164 (37.0%, 5 saves) — best trial yet**. Vocab: trial07 134 (31.4%), trial09 128 (27.8%).

  Saves+shares per view are 2–4× daily reels: people who see them value them, Instagram just doesn't widen them.

- Viewers leave early: avg watch 3.3–5.8 s, under half watch past 3 s.
