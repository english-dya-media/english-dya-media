# Hasan Kachal · حسن کچل — 4 parts

Source: an Iranian folk tale told in many versions (the lazy bald boy who outwits a div; the div whose life is kept
in a glass bottle, «شیشه‌ی عمر»; the captive girl with forty braids, «چهل‌گیس»). These motifs are old and shared by
many oral versions; this telling is ours. Do **not** use anything from Ali Hatami's 1970 film (its songs, lines,
scenes or look): it is under copyright. Short tale → 4 parts.

Tone: comic and warm, a children's tale that adults enjoy; quick jokes, big reactions. The div is scary-funny
(huge, hairy, horns, but a bit stupid). When his life-glass breaks he turns into smoke; no violence.

## Characters

| role | look | voice |
|---|---|---|
| NARRATOR | — | series narrator, a bit playful here |
| HASAN | bald head (shiny, a few hairs), round face, patched clothes, a cheeky smile; lazy at first, clever later | young, cheeky male |
| MOTHER | poor old widow, spinning wheel, patched headscarf, tired but loving | old, warm, nagging |
| DIV | huge, hairy, two horns, tusks, bare feet, a club; lives in a cave-castle full of treasure | booming, slow |
| CHEHEL GIS | the king's daughter, held by the div: forty long black braids, red dress, brave and clever | clear, clever female |
| KING | the girl's father (part 4), round, kind, big crown | jolly |
| villagers | as needed | — |

Places: a tiny mud house with a warm clay oven (tanoor) where Hasan lies all day; the village lane; a dark forest
and a mountain path; the div's cave-castle (bones? no: piles of pots, gold and junk); a tower room where Chehel Gis
sits; a big plane tree with a box in its roots; the king's palace and a wedding.

## Parts

| part | title | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lazy Hasan · حسن تنبل | Hook: in a dark forest a giant shadow roars and a small bald boy holds up an egg, trembling. Then: Hasan lies by the warm oven all day; his mother spins thread to buy bread. She begs him to work; he has jokes and excuses. One morning she takes the blanket away, gives him the last bread, an egg and a piece of cheese, and pushes him out of the door: "Don't come back with empty hands!" Turn: on a forest path a huge div blocks his way: "Who walks in MY forest?" |
| 2 | The Div and the Egg · دیو و تخم‌مرغ | Hasan, terrified, brags instead: he squeezes "a stone" (the egg) until "water" comes out; the div can't squeeze water from a real stone. A shouting contest (Hasan: "Cover your ears or they'll burst!"), a throwing contest (Hasan throws a bird that never comes down). The div decides Hasan is a hero and takes him home as his "brother", planning to kill him at night. Hasan puts a sack of straw in his bed and hides; the div beats the bed with his club. Morning: Hasan stretches: "A mosquito bit me last night." The div is terrified. Turn: Hasan hears a girl crying in the tower. |
| 3 | Chehel Gis · چهل‌گیس | In the tower: Chehel Gis, the king's daughter, stolen by the div. She says no sword can kill him: his life is kept in a glass bottle, but she doesn't know where. Plan: she sweetly asks the div where his life is; the div boasts it is in a box in the roots of the big plane tree. At night Hasan digs at the tree and finds the box. Turn: the div comes home early and sees Hasan holding the glass bottle. |
| 4 | The Glass of Life · شیشه‌ی عمر | Chase around the tree and the cave; Hasan is small and quick; the div begs; Hasan throws the bottle on a stone: it breaks and the div turns into black smoke that blows away. Hasan and Chehel Gis load a donkey with the div's gold and go home; his mother can't believe it. The king's joy; Hasan asks for Chehel Gis's hand; wedding with music. Ending: Hasan isn't lazy any more (well… almost: he still loves the warm oven). |
