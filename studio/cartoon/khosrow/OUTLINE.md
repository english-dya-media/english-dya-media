# Khosrow & Shirin · خسرو و شیرین — 10 parts

Source: Nizami Ganjavi, Khosrow o Shirin (c. 1180), in the Khamsa, on Ganjoor: https://ganjoor.net/nezami/5ganj/khosro-shirin/
About 6,500 couplets, the longest of these stories (and Farhad's tale inside it) → 10 parts. Dialogue is ours and
follows the poem's events. Nizami's famous question-and-answer between Khosrow and Farhad («بگفت از کجایی؟ بگفت از
دار آشنایی …») can be told in English in part 7, keeping its meaning.

Tone: a royal romance with music, horses and mountains; keep it warm and elegant. The spring scene in part 3 is
modest: Shirin, fully dressed, washes her long hair at a spring; Khosrow sees her from far away and looks away.
Deaths (Farhad, Khosrow, Shirin) are never shown: an axe that stops ringing, a lamp blown out, a tomb door closing.
Say that Shirin "stayed with him forever"; never show or describe how she died.

## Characters

| role | look | voice |
|---|---|---|
| NARRATOR | — | series narrator |
| KHOSROW (Parviz) | young prince → king: short black beard, tall jewelled crown, crimson and gold robe; a falcon or polo stick | confident baritone, softer with Shirin |
| SHIRIN | princess of Armenia, later queen: long black hair, light skin, crown of small gold leaves, sky-blue and white riding clothes; rides the black horse Shabdiz | clear, proud, warm female |
| SHAPUR | Khosrow's friend, a painter: young, cheerful, paint box and brushes | quick, cheerful |
| MAHIN BANU | queen of Armenia, Shirin's aunt, older, kind, silver crown | dignified female |
| FARHAD | master stone-carver: very tall, broad, simple clothes, axe and chisel, wild hair | deep, slow, gentle |
| MARYAM | daughter of the Roman emperor, golden hair, white and gold dress, a cross pendant | cool, proud |
| BARBAD, NAKISA | court musicians with lute and harp (part 9) | singing voices are hummed music only |
| OLD WOMAN | the false messenger (part 8) | creaky, sly |
| SHIRUYEH | Khosrow's son, cold eyes (parts 9–10) | flat, cold |
| ANUSHIRVAN | Khosrow's grandfather, in a dream (part 1), white light | old, echoing |
| BAHRAM CHUBIN | rebel general in black armour (part 4) | rough |

Places: the palace of Mada'in (the great arch of Ctesiphon); Shirin's green meadow in Armenia with tents and a tree;
the road and a spring in the hills; the polo field; the Roman court (part 5); Shirin's palace in a hot dry plain;
Mount Bisotun (a huge rock face Farhad cuts, with carved figures); Shirin's castle on a snowy night; a dark prison;
a royal tomb.

## Parts

| part | title | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shapur the Painter · شاپور نقاش | Hook: night on Mount Bisotun, a giant man strikes the rock and calls "Shirin!" (from part 7). Young prince Khosrow; in a dream his grandfather Anushirvan promises him four gifts: a beautiful wife, a black horse faster than wind, a great throne and a musician. His friend Shapur, a painter, describes Shirin, niece of Mahin Banu, queen of Armenia. Khosrow falls in love with a description. Turn: "Go and bring me her heart." Shapur rides to Armenia with his brushes. |
| 2 | Three Portraits · سه تصویر | Shirin and her ladies picnic in a meadow. Shapur secretly hangs a portrait of Khosrow on a tree; Shirin sees it and can't look away; her ladies hide it. Again the next day, and a third time. Shapur appears, tells her who the young man is and gives her Khosrow's ring: "Ride to Mada'in." Turn: before dawn Shirin takes Shabdiz, the fastest horse, and rides away alone. |
| 3 | The Spring · چشمه | At the same time Khosrow has to leave Mada'in (his father is angry with him) and rides towards Armenia. On the road he sees, far away, a girl washing her long hair at a spring beside a black horse; their eyes meet; neither knows the other; she rides on. Shirin reaches Mada'in, Khosrow reaches Armenia; Mahin Banu welcomes him. Shapur arrives: "The girl at the spring was Shirin!" Turn: a rider brings news: King Hormoz is dead; Khosrow must go back and be king. |
| 4 | The Rebel General · بهرام چوبین | Khosrow is crowned, but the general Bahram Chubin rebels and Khosrow has to flee. In Armenia at last he meets Shirin (a hunt, then a feast and a polo game: Shirin and her ladies play too, and play well). They are in love, but Shirin says: "Win back your throne first. I'll be your wife by law, not before." Turn: Khosrow leaves for Rome to ask the emperor for an army. |
| 5 | Maryam · مریم | The emperor gives Khosrow an army and his daughter Maryam; she makes him promise to take no other wife. Khosrow wins back his throne. In Armenia, Mahin Banu dies and Shirin becomes queen; then she leaves her kingdom and lives alone near Mada'in in a palace in a hot, dry plain. Turn: she needs milk brought from far pastures; someone says: "There is a man who can cut stone like wax: Farhad." |
| 6 | Farhad · فرهاد | Farhad comes; Shirin speaks to him from behind a curtain and he falls in love with her voice. In one month he cuts a stone channel to bring the milk. She offers him her earrings; he lays them at her feet and walks into the mountains, mad with love. People talk. Turn: Khosrow, jealous, sends for Farhad. |
| 7 | Bisotun · بیستون | Khosrow and Farhad: the famous questions and answers ("Where are you from?" "From the land of friendship." …). Khosrow can't buy him or frighten him, so he sets a task: "Cut a road through Mount Bisotun and I will leave Shirin to you." Farhad cuts the mountain day and night and carves pictures of Shirin and Khosrow in the rock. Shirin visits him; her horse stumbles and Farhad carries her and the horse back on his shoulders. Turn: Khosrow's advisers whisper: "Send him false news." |
| 8 | The False News · خبر دروغ | An old woman climbs to Farhad: "Shirin is dead." Farhad's axe falls silent (we never see him die); his story ends on the mountain. Shirin mourns. Khosrow sends her a cool letter of condolence. Then Maryam dies, and Shirin sends Khosrow the same kind of letter. Turn: on a snowy night Khosrow rides to Shirin's castle. |
| 9 | The Snowy Night · شب برفی | Shirin won't open the gate: he left her for Maryam (and, Nizami adds, for Shakar of Isfahan); she wants respect and marriage, not visits. They argue under the snow; he leaves angry. Later the musicians Barbad and Nakisa sing each one's heart to the other (music, no lyrics). They make peace and marry; with Shirin's advice Khosrow becomes a just king. Turn: years later his son Shiruyeh watches from the shadows. |
| 10 | Shiruyeh · شیرویه | Shiruyeh seizes the throne and puts Khosrow in prison; Shirin stays with him. One night a shadow enters the prison and the lamp goes out: Khosrow is gone (not shown). Shiruyeh asks Shirin to marry him; she agrees on one condition: to see Khosrow's tomb first. She goes into the tomb, the door closes, and she stays with him forever (nothing shown). Ending: narrator on love and loyalty. |
